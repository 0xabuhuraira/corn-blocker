# Corn Blocker – Privacy Policy

Corn Blocker does **not** collect, store off-device, sell, or transmit any personal data, browsing history, or search queries.

- URLs and search terms are checked **locally** in your browser against the blocklists. Nothing about them leaves your device.
- Your settings (custom keywords, domains, allowlist) are stored in your browser's local extension storage.
- The only network request is downloading the public domain blocklist from `raw.githubusercontent.com`. This request contains no information about you or your browsing.
- The extension requests access to all sites so it can check page addresses and search boxes. This access is used for blocking only.
- No analytics, no ads, no remote code.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
